# Vertex Timeline — May 2026 through December 2028

Written 2026-09-27. This file lists what Vertex has done since the case was written (May 2026), followed by the milestones expected through the end of 2028.

Every entry carries one of four labels:
- **Reported:** it happened, and a source confirms it.
- **Company guidance:** Vertex has said it expects this.
- **Analyst expectation:** equity analysts or outside observers expect this.
- **Team projection:** the team's own estimate, derived from the other entries. Treat these as assumptions, not facts.

Recheck any entry before it goes on a slide.
