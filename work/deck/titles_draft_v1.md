# Titles-Only Draft, Version 1 (Sept 27, 2026)

This draft states the first hypothesis (decision log entry D-003) as slide titles. Bracketed values such as [N] are numbers the research and the model will supply. Read the titles in order: they should make the whole argument by themselves. The third column shows the evidence each title needs, so gaps are visible before the research starts.

The hypothesis may change. If the model picks a different combination, these titles change with it, and the change is logged.

## What the titles show about gaps

- Slides 3, 4, 5, and 9 depend on one set of numbers: the US label-eligible count, its primary care share, and today's testing volume. No sponsor report gives the US-only count, so WS-A must estimate it, with a range.
- Slide 8 depends on a legal question. If rules bar a sales force from unbranded test-and-refer calls before approval, the contracted team starts at approval, and the staging on slide 12 changes.
- Slide 6 needs a value for each displaced nephrology call. That value needs povetacicept's net price, which is not public before its Nov 30, 2026 decision; the model will carry it as an assumption with a range.
- Slide 10 compares the recommendation with the runner-up. The runner-up is unknown until the model runs.
