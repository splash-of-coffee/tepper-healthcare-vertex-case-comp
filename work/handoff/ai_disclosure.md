# AI Disclosure — Two Versions (draft, Sept 27, 2026)

The organizers gave no guidance on AI disclosure. The team decides whether to use either version, and where. The text below describes the process as planned; the team updates it before submission if the process changes.

## Version 1: one paragraph for the submission email or an appendix footer

Our team used AI tools (Anthropic's Claude) for research support, first drafts of text and slides, building the financial model's spreadsheet from our assumptions, and checking numbers and wording. The four team members chose the strategy, set and checked every assumption in the model, verified each cited fact against its original source, and approved every slide. The recommendation and its conclusions are the team's own, and we can explain and defend each number in the deck.

## Version 2: text for the optional 17th slide

**Title:** The team used AI tools for research, drafting, and checks, and the team set the strategy and checked every number

**What AI tools did**
- Searched public sources and summarized the sponsor's analyst reports, with page citations
- Drafted slide text, charts, and the spreadsheet model from the team's assumptions
- Checked numbers against the model and wording against our plain-language standard

**What the team did**
- Chose the recommendation at a strategy review on Oct 1, 2026
- Set every model assumption, and each teammate recomputed one model section by hand
- Verified each cited fact against its original source
- Approved every slide and owns every conclusion

**Tools:** Anthropic Claude (Claude Code). No case materials or analyst reports were uploaded to any public service.
