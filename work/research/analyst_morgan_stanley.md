# Morgan Stanley report on inaxaplin (April 19, 2026)

Source file: `materials\analyst-reports\AMKD Reports - MBA_[overview] Morgan Stanley 4.19.26.pdf`, 21 PDF pages. Page references are PDF pages, written p.N. Pages 15 to 21 hold disclosures and the coverage list, and p.13 is blank. All figures are as printed unless marked "calculated."

Terms used in this file:
- AMKD: APOL1-mediated kidney disease, kidney disease driven by two high-risk variants of the APOL1 gene.
- FSGS: focal segmental glomerulosclerosis, a scarring pattern in the kidney's filtering units. APOL1-FSGS is the subgroup that inaxaplin's first trial (Phase 2a) studied.
- UPCR and UACR: urine protein-to-creatinine ratio and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, two urine measures of kidney damage.
- eGFR: estimated glomerular filtration rate, a blood-test estimate of kidney function, in mL/min/1.73 m².
- ESKD: end-stage kidney disease.
- POS: probability of success, the chance the analyst assigns that the drug reaches the market. Risk-adjusted sales are forecast sales multiplied by the POS.
- DCF: discounted cash flow valuation. WACC: weighted average cost of capital, the discount rate in the DCF.
- WAC: wholesale acquisition cost, the list price. GTN: gross-to-net, the percent discount from list price to net price.
- BLA: biologics license application. PRV: priority review voucher, which shortens FDA review to six months.
- IgAN: IgA nephropathy, the first indication for povetacicept ("pove"). T2D: type 2 diabetes. CKD: chronic kidney disease.
- IDMC: independent data monitoring committee.
- ACE inhibitor and ARB: angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor and angiotensin receptor blocker, standard blood-pressure drugs that also lower urine protein.
- CMS: Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services. ICD-10: the diagnosis code set used on US medical claims.
- DPN: diabetic peripheral neuropathy, a pain indication for Journavx. EPS: earnings per share. P/E: share price divided by EPS.

## 2. AMKD details

### Launch year
- The report states no launch year for inaxaplin.
- The report expects AMPLITUDE Phase 2/3 data in "4Q/1Q" (p.1), described elsewhere as "late 2026 or early 2027" (p.1, p.12). It says these data "could support an FDA filing" (p.1).

### Approval path and the endpoints the FDA wants
- The report does not say whether it expects accelerated or full approval.
- Morgan Stanley writes: "Per VRTX, eGFR (not UPCR) data is FDA's focus of the Ph2/3 interim analysis" (p.12).
- The AMPLITUDE design graphic shows a 48-week interim analysis on "eGFR slope" and "% change in UPCR from baseline," and a 2-year final analysis on "eGFR slope" and "Time to composite clinical outcome" (p.12).
- Morgan Stanley counts "a stat sig improvement on eGFR over placebo" as a positive Phase 3 outcome (p.12). It also thinks "at least 40% UPCR reduction from baseline is likely positive," citing Egbuna (NEJM 2023) data from nearly 30,000 patients (p.12).
- AMPLIFIED, the separate Phase 2 study, has a primary endpoint of percent change in UACR at week 13 (p.10).

### US list price, net price, and gross-to-net
- Morgan Stanley models "$135k annual net price" for inaxaplin (p.5). The report does not label this price as US or worldwide.
- The report cites TVTX's Filspari as a "Potential pricing analog," with "an annual WAC of ~$150k, and the GTN is in the mid-20% range" (p.5).
- The report gives no inaxaplin list price and no inaxaplin-specific GTN.
- Applying a 25% GTN, the middle of "mid-20%," gives the analog a net price of $150,000 × (1 − 0.25) = $112,500 (calculated). The $135,000 net price Morgan Stanley models is $22,500 above that figure (calculated).

### Patients on therapy
- The report states: "Our 2035 sales assume ~38k patients on therapy (25% of the 150k patients)" (p.5). The 150k is Vertex's US-plus-EU estimate of primary AMKD patients (p.1, p.5).
- The report gives no patient counts for other years.

### Probability of success
- Morgan Stanley assigns inaxaplin a 50% POS (p.1, p.5).

### Discount rate
- Morgan Stanley derives its price target from a DCF "Using A WACC Of 10% And A Terminal Growth Rate Of 2% Post 2040" (p.14). This rate applies to the whole-company DCF.

### Population definitions
- **US versus US-plus-EU.** Vertex estimates 150k US-plus-EU patients with primary AMKD and 100k US-plus-EU patients with AMKD and moderate proteinuria or diabetes, 250k in total (p.1, p.5).
- **US only.** Morgan Stanley estimates "~1.2mn Americans with AMKD." It cites Srinivasan (Kidney Medicine 2025): about 13% of African Americans, 6mn people, carry two high-risk alleles, and 20% of them develop kidney disease (p.5). Multiplying 6mn by 20% gives 1.2mn (calculated).
- **US only, Maze's figure.** Maze "estimates at least 250k AMKD patients in the US, for which diabetic patients make up 40%" (p.5). Forty percent of 250k is 100k (calculated). The page 5 pie chart shows diabetics, hypertensive non-diabetics, other CKD, and FSGS slices without percentages (p.5).
- **Primary AMKD (AMPLITUDE).** Primary AMKD includes APOL1-FSGS (p.1). AMPLITUDE enrolls patients with UPCR 0.7 to under 10 g/g and eGFR 25 to under 75 mL/min/1.73 m² (p.12).
- **AMPLIFIED populations.** AMPLIFIED has two cohorts "that do not overlap with the population of AMPLITUDE": AMKD with moderate proteinuria (UACR 100-420 mg/g), and AMKD with T2D (p.1, p.10). The design graphic shows about 20 patients per cohort at 45 mg once daily for 13 weeks. It labels the T2D cohort "Moderate to severe Proteinuria," with UACR marks at 100, 420, and 6,000 mg/g (p.10).
- **Market expansion.** Vertex says AMPLIFIED "has the potential to expand the addressable market to 250k from 150k US+EU patients" (p.1).
- **FSGS share.** APOL1-FSGS "only represents 4% of the AMKD population" (p.1).
- **Subpopulations.** The report lists three: primary AMKD including APOL1-FSGS, AMKD with moderate proteinuria, and AMKD with comorbidities such as T2D and HIV (p.4).
- **Diabetic versus non-diabetic.** The report writes: "Per VRTX, the role of APOL1 is less clear in AMKD patients with comorbidities (e.g., T2D)" (p.10). The report adds that "VRTX has recently stated that the biology for AMKD patients with T2D is less clear" (p.1). A CRIC study panel shows higher progression risk for APOL1 high-risk versus low-risk genotypes in both groups: hazard ratio 1.27 (p = 0.098) in non-diabetics and 1.52 (p = 0.002) in diabetics (p.11).

### Diagnosis, testing, primary care, referral, biopsy, payer criteria, ICD-10 codes
- The report states: "Majority of these patients are not diagnosed. VRTX offers free APOL1 genotyping." (p.5)
- The report states: "CMS also approved ICD-10 codes for AMKD, effective 10/1/2025." (p.5) The report does not list the codes.
- The AMPLIFIED design includes an "Optional Genotype Screening visit" before day −28 (p.10).
- The report does not discuss primary care, referral, biopsy, or payer criteria.

## 3. Povetacicept and the kidney business
- Morgan Stanley writes that "VRTX's nephrology vertical is taking shape in 2026, driven by two late-stage assets - Pove and Inaxaplin" (p.1).
- **Launch timing.** Vertex completed the BLA submission for potential accelerated approval of pove on 3/31 (p.1). Vertex expects a six-month review from acceptance because it used a PRV (p.1).
- **Sales.** Morgan Stanley models pove 2035 risk-adjusted sales of $6.5bn at 90% POS, against a consensus of $3.1bn (p.1).
- **Pricing and sales force.** The report gives no pove price and does not discuss a sales force for pove or inaxaplin.
- **Other kidney programs.** The report discusses no kidney program other than pove and inaxaplin.
- **Maze Therapeutics (MZE829).** Morgan Stanley does not cover Maze (p.5). The report summarizes the Maze Phase 2 data on p.9:
  - MZE829 is an APOL1 inhibitor dosed 250 mg orally once daily, with "Potential to use a higher dose in a pivotal study."
  - Patients had to be on a stable background kidney regimen for at least 8 weeks.
  - One-third of evaluable patients have FSGS, and 42% have diabetes.
  - Baseline UACR is 1.1 g/g overall, 1.4 g/g without diabetes, and 0.8 g/g with diabetes. Baseline UPCR is 1.7, 2.1, and 1.4 g/g in the same groups.
  - The graphic reports a 36% mean uACR reduction at 12 weeks across both groups and a 50% rate of patients with at least a 30% reduction.
  - Patients without diabetes show a 49% mean uACR reduction and a 57% rate of at least 30% reduction. The FSGS subset shows a 62% mean reduction.

## 5. Risks, and the view on AMKD efficacy outside APOL1-FSGS
- **Upside risks.** The report lists "Casgevy takes a significant market share," "Journavx takes a significant share," "Inaxaplin has a positive Ph3 interim readout," and "Journavx has a positive Ph3 readout in DPN" (p.14).
- **Downside risks.** The report lists "Casgevy/Journavx fails to take meaningful market share," "Journavx Ph3 trial in DPN fails," "Inaxaplin fails in Ph3," and "Pove fails to take meaningful share in IgAN" (p.14).
- **Safety.** The report calls inaxaplin's safety "acceptable" but says "longer-term data from a larger population is needed to fully derisk the profile" (p.8). In the Phase 2a safety table (N = 16), 15 patients (94%) had an adverse event, 1 (6%) had a serious adverse event, none stopped treatment, and 4 (25%) had headache (p.8). The IDMC reviewed unblinded data from 66 AMPLITUDE Phase 2 patients. It recommended adding adolescents aged 10 to 17 and selecting the 45 mg once-daily dose (p.8).
- **View on efficacy outside APOL1-FSGS.** The report states that "a key question is whether the drug will work beyond APOL1-mediated FSGS" (p.1).
  - Morgan Stanley expects "a limited read-through from AMPLIFIED to AMPLITUDE" because the populations do not overlap (p.11).
  - It believes "some investors may look to Cohort 1 (pure AMKD with moderate proteinuria) to gauge POS of AMPLITUDE" (p.11).
  - It cites Egbuna (NEJM 2023): a 30% UACR reduction "translated to a 27% reduction in the risk of a composite clinical endpoint of ESKD" (p.11).
- **AMPLIFIED stock scenarios.** The report gives these expected stock moves for the AMPLIFIED readout (p.2):

| AMPLIFIED outcome | Expected VRTX stock move |
|---|---|
| UACR cut by at least 30% in both cohorts, no new safety signal | up 5-10% |
| UACR cut by at least 30% in the moderate-proteinuria cohort only, no new safety signal | up low-to-mid single digits |
| UACR cut under 30% in both cohorts, no new safety signal | down low-to-mid single digits |
| New safety signal | down high single digits |

- **Phase 2a efficacy in APOL1-FSGS (p.6).** Dosing was 15 mg once daily for 2 weeks, then 45 mg once daily for 11 weeks. Across 13 patients, mean UPCR fell 47.6% from 2.21 to 1.27 g/g at week 13, which equals a 43% UACR reduction. The nephrotic-range group (N = 3) fell 47.7%, and the subnephrotic-range group (N = 10) fell 47.5%. Half the patients (50%) took an ACE inhibitor and 44% took an ARB.
- **Disease course.** Vertex reports that African American AMKD patients lose eGFR at 6.55 mL/min/1.73 m² per year, compared with 3.63 for African Americans with CKD and no APOL1 risk variants (p.4).
