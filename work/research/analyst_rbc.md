# RBC Capital Markets report on Vertex (June 4, 2026)

Source file: `materials\analyst-reports\AMKD Reports - MBA_[bear] RBC 6.4.26.pdf`, 15 PDF pages. Page references are PDF pages, written p.N. Pages 1 to 6 hold the analysis, p.7 is the income statement, p.8 is the milestone and pipeline table, and pages 9 to 15 hold valuation notes and disclosures. The income statement is in $ thousands; this file shows those figures in $M. All figures are as printed unless marked "calculated."

Terms used in this file:
- AMKD: APOL1-mediated kidney disease, kidney disease driven by two high-risk variants of the APOL1 gene.
- FSGS: focal segmental glomerulosclerosis, a scarring pattern in the kidney's filtering units.
- uPCR and uACR: urine protein-to-creatinine ratio and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, two urine measures of kidney damage. "Proteinuria" means protein in the urine.
- eGFR: estimated glomerular filtration rate, a blood-test estimate of kidney function. "eGFR slope" is the rate of change in eGFR over time.
- PoS: probability of success, the chance the analyst assigns that the drug reaches the market. "Risk-adjusted" sales are forecast sales multiplied by the PoS.
- DCF: discounted cash flow valuation. KOL: key opinion leader, a physician expert the analyst interviews.
- WW: worldwide. ADA: anti-drug antibodies.
- IgAN: IgA nephropathy, the first indication for povetacicept ("pove"). pMN: primary membranous nephropathy. ADPKD: autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease. gMG: generalized myasthenia gravis. wAIHA: warm autoimmune hemolytic anemia.
- CF: cystic fibrosis. SwCl: sweat chloride, the CF biomarker. DPN: diabetic peripheral neuropathy. EPS: earnings per share.
- Accelerated approval: FDA approval based on an early measure likely to predict benefit, with confirmation required later. Full approval: approval based on the confirmed clinical endpoint.

### Approval path and the endpoints the FDA wants
- **Accelerated filing.** The milestone table lists a "Potential accelerated approval filing in AMKD" for 2027 (p.8).
- **Interim endpoints.** RBC writes that "the statistical design of the interim tests both proteinuria reductions and slope differences between the inaxaplin and placebo arms in eGFR trajectory" (p.5). Enrollment in the 48-week interim cohort "had completed in 2H25," with an "early-'27 readout" (p.5).
- **eGFR timing.** RBC's KOLs say "longer follow-up (at least 2 years) may be needed to robustly observe an eGFR slope separation" (p.5). Vertex "believes enrolled the population would have 3-8mL/min reductions over one year" (p.5). RBC expects the 2-year data "likely available in early to mid-2028" (p.5).
- **Full approval path.** RBC writes: "We believe the stock has already largely discounted a non-accelerated approval outcome at the 48-week interim" (p.6). It adds that "the path to full approval on a 2-year eGFR endpoint remains well-supported by our KOL conversations and the precedent set by sparsentan in FSGS" (p.6).

### Probability of success
- The report does not state a PoS for inaxaplin.
- The report gives two fair-value sensitivities. RBC writes that "increasing PoS to 100% would increase our DCF-based fair value by +9%" (p.5). It writes that removing inaxaplin "from our model would decrease our DCF-based fair value by 6%" (p.6).
- If inaxaplin's value scales in proportion to PoS, these sensitivities imply a PoS of 6% ÷ (6% + 9%) = 40% (calculated). The 40% rests on that proportional-scaling assumption and on rounded inputs, so it is a calculation, not a printed figure.
- For comparison, RBC prints a 30% PoS for pove in pMN (p.4) and an estimated 40% PoS for suzetrigine in DPN (p.6).

### Discount rate
- RBC's $543 price target is "derived from a DCF analysis of the base business, with a 9.0% discount rate and a 2.5% terminal growth rate" (p.9, p.14). This rate applies to the whole-company DCF.

### Population definitions
- **Primary AMKD and the diabetic subset.** RBC writes that "the diabetic subset represents a sizable proportion of the real-world AMKD population (+100K pts, as per company, on top of 150K AMPLITUDE population)" (p.5). The report does not say whether these counts are US or US-plus-EU.
- **AMPLIFIED populations.** RBC describes AMPLIFIED as "a ph.IIb open-label study of inaxaplin in AMKD patients with more modest proteinuria" than AMPLITUDE patients, "or who have diabetes" (p.4, p.5).
- **Proteinuria ranges.** RBC believes AMPLIFIED "is enrolling pts with more modest baseline protenuria (100-400 mg/g)" [sic]. It notes that Maze's study "included pts with uACR of 300-3,000 mg/g" (p.5).
- **Diabetic versus non-diabetic.** RBC describes diabetic AMKD patients as a group "who likely has multi-factorial influences for their kidney dysfunction" (p.5). It back-calculates that Maze's drug "likely produced only a ~15-20% reduction in proteinuria" in diabetic AMKD (p.5).

- **FDA precedent.** RBC cites "VERA's recent alignment with FDA on an earlier eGFR analysis (which VRTX could potentially do as well)" (p.3).
- **Other kidney programs.** The Phase IIb/III OLYMPUS trial compares pove with tacrolimus in pMN in 176 patients. Its primary endpoint is "complete clinical remission at Week 104" (p.3). RBC anticipates a Phase IIb readout in 2H26, possibly at a major medical meeting, "potentially ASN, October 21-25" (p.3, p.4). Pove's RUBY-3 results at 48 weeks were 100% serologic remission, an 82% UPCR reduction in 5 pMN patients, 40% complete clinical remission (UPCR under 0.5 g/g), and an eGFR change of −0.3 mL/min/1.73 m² (p.3, p.4). The milestone table also lists "Ph. II ADPKD AGLOW study data" for VX-407 in 2027 (p.8), and pove gMG Phase II data for 2H26 or later (p.8).
- **Maze Therapeutics.** Maze "had recently demonstrated 61.8% uACR reduction in non-diabetic AMKD FSGS patients (n=4)" (p.5). RBC compares this with "VRTX's earlier 47.6% uPCR reduction from a ph.II in a similar population (n=13)" (p.5). RBC notes that "MAZE's data set was highly variable and sample sizes small" (p.5). Maze's data "showed ~20% lesser proteinuria reductions for the fuller non-diabetic group as compared to the FSGS subset" (p.5). The report does not name MZE829.

## 5. Risks, and the view on AMKD efficacy outside APOL1-FSGS
- **Named risks.** RBC lists "lower-than-expected commercial uptake of Alyftrek, pricing pressure in CF, competition, and emergence of safety issues from pipeline programs like povetacicept, suzetrigine, or inaxaplin" (p.9, p.14).
- **Downside scenario ($400).** The scenario includes "Emergence of competition reducing long-term market share," a safety signal for suzetrigine, pove, inaxaplin, or zimislecel/'264, and "eroding pricing power" (p.9).
- **CF competitor.** Sionna's SION-719 Phase IIa readout is due in the summer (p.1). RBC gives a 70% likelihood to a SwCl change under 10 mmol, with VRTX up more than 10% (p.2). It gives 15% to a change of about 10 mmol (+5-10%), 10% to a change over 10 mmol (−5%), and 5% to a change far over 10 mmol (−10%) (p.2).
- **View on efficacy outside APOL1-FSGS.** RBC expects AMPLITUDE proteinuria reductions of 40-50% even with some loss of effect beyond FSGS (p.5).
  - RBC writes that "even allowing for some degradation in going beyond just FSGS pts ... we still believe proteinuria reductions could come in at the 40-50% range" in AMPLITUDE (p.5).
  - RBC writes that "Translation to eGFR is a bit more complicated" given the "heterogeneity of the population" (p.5).
  - RBC calls the AMPLIFIED groups "likely more challenging populations to show as robust an effect in" (p.5). It notes that Vertex "has implied they would like to see ~30% proteinuria reductions" (p.5).
  - RBC believes "fundamental readthroughs to AMPLITUDE should be limited" from AMPLIFIED (p.5).
- **AMKD stock scenarios.** RBC gives these expected stock moves:

|---|---|---|
