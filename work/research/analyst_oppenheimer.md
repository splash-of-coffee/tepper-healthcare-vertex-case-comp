# Oppenheimer report on Vertex (February 13, 2026)

Source file: `materials\analyst-reports\AMKD Reports - MBA_[bull] Oppenheimer 2.13.26.pdf`, 24 PDF pages. Page references are PDF pages, written p.N. Pages 3 to 20 are slide-format exhibits, p.21 is the model, and pages 22 to 24 hold disclosures. All figures are as printed unless marked "calculated."

Terms used in this file:
- AMKD: APOL1-mediated kidney disease, kidney disease driven by two high-risk variants of the APOL1 gene.
- FSGS: focal segmental glomerulosclerosis, a scarring pattern in the kidney's filtering units. NDKD: non-diabetic kidney disease. CKD: chronic kidney disease.
- UPCR: urine protein-to-creatinine ratio, a urine measure of kidney damage. eGFR: estimated glomerular filtration rate, a blood-test estimate of kidney function.
- PoC: proof of concept. IDMC: independent data monitoring committee. QD: once daily.
- POS: probability of success, the chance the analyst assigns that the drug reaches the market. "P-adj" (probability-adjusted) values are multiplied by the POS.
- NPV: net present value, future cash flows discounted to today's dollars. PV: present value. SOTP: sum-of-the-parts valuation, which adds the NPV of each product.
- BD: business development, meaning acquisitions and licensing deals.
- DCF: discounted cash flow valuation. WACC: weighted average cost of capital, the DCF discount rate. Residual growth rate: the growth rate applied after the forecast years.
- BLA: biologics license application. PDUFA date: the FDA's target date for an approval decision.
- IgAN: IgA nephropathy, the first indication for povetacicept ("pove"). pMN: primary membranous nephropathy. gMG: generalized myasthenia gravis. wAIHA: warm autoimmune hemolytic anemia.
- CF: cystic fibrosis. LOE: loss of exclusivity. EPS: earnings per share.

### Probability of success and value
- Oppenheimer assigns inaxaplin a 60% POS (p.19).
- The sum-of-the-parts table gives inaxaplin an unadjusted NPV of $20,007M, a probability-adjusted NPV of $12,004M, $47 per share, and 9% of total value (p.19).
- Multiplying $20,007M by 0.60 gives $12,004M, and dividing by 256.5M diluted shares gives $46.80 per share (calculated). Both results match the printed figures after rounding.

### Discount rate
- "We assume a WACC of 8% and a residual growth rate of -1%" (p.2).
- The DCF table shows an 8.0% discount rate and a −1.0% terminal growth rate. It gives an NPV of $69,255M, a terminal value of $154,223M, a present value of terminal value of $52,507M, debt of $1,753M, cash of $18,604M, and an equity value of $138,613M, for a $540 price target (p.20).
- The DCF sensitivity grid runs from a 6.5% to a 9.0% discount rate and from −4% to +2% terminal growth. Its values range from $438 per share (9.0%, −4%) to $880 per share (6.5%, +2%) (p.20).
- These rates apply to the whole-company DCF.

### Population definitions
- **US plus EU.** "VRTX estimates a total of ~250K patients in US and EU with APOL1-demidated kidney disease (AMKD)" [sic] (p.10).
- **Genotype prevalence.** Oppenheimer cites "A recent APOL1 genotyping global study" showing that "About 44%-50% of focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (FSGS) patients carry two APOL1 risk variants" (p.10). It adds that "21-31% of nondiabetic kidney disease (NDKD) patients carry two APOL1 risk variants" (p.10).
- **Primary AMKD (AMPLITUDE).** The report states that "AMPLITUDE enrolls a broader proteinuria kidney disease population carrying two high-risk APOL1 variants" than the FSGS-only Phase 2a (p.12).
- **AMPLIFIED.** "A separate Ph2 AMPLIFIED basket study is designed to evaluate inaxaplin in an expanded population of AMKD patients" (p.12). The catalyst table lists AMPLIFIED data for "Mid-26" with "Medium" importance (p.17).
- **Diabetic versus non-diabetic.** The report does not split the population by diabetes status, apart from the NDKD genotype figure above.
- The report does not split the ~250K figure between US and EU, or between primary AMKD and the AMPLIFIED populations.

### Diagnosis, testing, primary care, referral, biopsy, payer criteria, ICD-10 codes
- Oppenheimer cites a retrospective study (Shah et al., ASN 2025). It says AMKD patients "progressed to dialysis and kidney transplant significantly faster than matched patients with other forms of CKD." It adds that this finding highlights "the need for APOL1 testing" (p.10).
- The study's table (645 patients per group) gives these dialysis rates for AMKD versus other CKD: 23.8% vs. 17.8% at 3 years, 34.1% vs. 23.3% at 5 years, 50.6% vs. 31.5% at 10 years, and 59.9% vs. 37.0% at 15 years (p.10).
- The same table gives these kidney transplant rates: 25.6% vs. 19.3% at 3 years, 40.7% vs. 25.5% at 5 years, 60.5% vs. 36.9% at 10 years, and 66.4% vs. 50.2% at 15 years (p.10).
- The report does not discuss Vertex-sponsored free APOL1 testing, primary care, referral, payer criteria, or ICD-10 codes for AMKD.
- The report mentions biopsy only for pMN, a povetacicept indication: "diagnosis is often confirmed through biopsy" (p.9).

## 3. Povetacicept and the kidney business
- **Renal franchise.** Oppenheimer sees 2026 "as an inflection year for VRTX's emerging renal franchise" (p.1). It expects pove and inaxaplin to contribute "~$6.6B in revenue by 2035" (p.1).
- **Filing and launch timing.** Vertex "initiated a rolling BLA submission for pove in 4Q25" and planned to complete it "in 1H26 with an intent to use a priority review voucher" (p.6). The development-timeline table lists the pove BLA filing for 1H26 and the FDA decision for 2H26 (p.6). The sum-of-the-parts table lists an expected pove launch of 2027E (p.19).
- **Development speed.** The RAINIER Phase 3 trial enrolled about 605 participants in about 15 months (p.6). The trial started in September 2024 and completed enrollment in November 2025 (p.6). Oppenheimer estimates 25 to 27 months from Phase 3 start to potential approval, against about 37 months for atacicept and about 44 months for sibeprenlimab (p.6).
- **Interim bar.** Oppenheimer titles its p.5 exhibit "We Expect at Least 45% UPCR Reduction at Week-36 for Pove" (p.5). Its scenario table (p.5) assigns these likelihoods and stock moves:

| Pove UPCR reduction at week 36 | Safety | Likelihood | Stock move |
|---|---|---|---|
| over 50% | consistent | 30% | over +10% |
| 45-50% | consistent | 40% | +5-10% |
| 40-45% | consistent | 20% | flat |
| under 40% | or safety signals emerge | 10% | −10% |

- **Sales and value.** The sum-of-the-parts table gives pove (IgAN and pMN) a peak sales estimate of $7,936M, an unadjusted NPV of $26,183M, a 63% POS, a probability-adjusted NPV of $16,364M, and $64 per share (p.19). The model shows pove sales of $0.0 in 2026E, $51.0M in 2027E, and $212.5M in 2028E, probability-adjusted (p.21).
- **Pricing and sales force.** The report gives no pove price. It does not discuss a sales force. It credits "VRTX's strong execution and commercial capability" for pove's position (p.1).
- **Profile (p.4, p.7).** Pove is dosed 80 mg subcutaneously every 4 weeks. In the RUBY-3 study (N = 21), proteinuria fell 57% at week 36 and 64% at week 48 (p.4). Pove is a "Once-monthly at-home subQ injection with auto-injector" with a volume under 0.5 mL (p.7).
- **Competitors in IgAN (p.4).** Atacicept (Vera) had a PDUFA date of 7/7/2026 and a 46% proteinuria cut at week 36 (42% placebo-adjusted). Sibeprenlimab (Otsuka) was "Approved in Nov. 25" with a 50% cut at week 40 (51% placebo-adjusted). Telitacicept (Remegen/Vor) had a "BLA filed in China" and a 59% cut at week 39 (55% placebo-adjusted).
- **Other kidney programs.** Vertex started the Phase 2/3 OLYMPUS program for pove in pMN in 3Q25 (p.8). The Phase 2 portion should complete enrollment in 2026 (p.9). Oppenheimer cites "Roughly ~40K pMN patients in the US" and "No FDA-approved therapies for pMN" (p.9). Vertex also plans non-kidney pove studies in gMG, with a Phase 2 start in 1H26, and in wAIHA (p.8).
- **Maze Therapeutics.** The report does not mention Maze or MZE829.

## 4. Company-level figures
- **2025 revenue.** Revenue was $12.0B in 2025A (p.1). The model gives total revenues (non-GAAP) of $12,001.3M and probability-adjusted product sales of $11,970.6M (p.21).
- **2026 guidance.** Vertex's "FY26 total revenue guidance of $12.95-13.10B" was "largely in line with the consensus estimate of $13.0B" (p.1). The report states that "Non-CF products are expected to contribute at least $500M" (p.1).
- **Oppenheimer revenue estimates.** Oppenheimer estimates revenue of $12.3B for 2026E (prior $12.2B) and $13.4B for 2027E (prior $13.5B) (p.1). The model shows $12,301.8M for 2026E, $13,449.3M for 2027E, and $14,607.6M for 2028E (p.21). Total revenue reaches "~$26B in 2035 (vs. $12B in 2025)" (p.2).
- **EPS.** EPS is $18.40 for 2025A, $18.05 for 2026E (prior $19.55), and $20.20 for 2027E (prior $21.92) (p.1).
- **CF share of revenue.** Non-CF programs make up "current ~1.6% for 2025" of revenue and are "expected to contribute ~50% of total revenues by 2035" (p.18). The model's 2025 CF rows (Trikafta/Kaftrio $10,312.7M, other CF $644.7M, CF pipeline $837.8M) sum to $11,795.2M, which is 98.3% of $12,001.3M (calculated from p.21).
- **CF outlook.** Oppenheimer expects CF revenue of "~$13.4B revenues in 2030E (vs. consensus $15.6B)" (p.14). Vertex's CF medicines "could target ~95% of all CF patients" (p.14).
- **Exclusivity.** The report gives no exclusivity dates. It sees "no near-term concerns around LOE or competitive pressure" (p.2). It calls CF "a solid foundation for the business well into the late 2030s" (p.1).
- **Other.** Oppenheimer believes Vertex "has capacity for additional BD transactions in the range of $5-10B" (p.18). Casgevy revenue was $116M in 2025, and more than 60 patients had received infusions by year-end 2025 (p.16).
- **Market data.** The cover lists market capitalization of $117,984.3M, 254.0M shares outstanding, and long-term debt of $1,753.0M (p.1).

## 5. Risks, and the view on AMKD efficacy outside APOL1-FSGS
- **Named risks.** Oppenheimer names two risks (p.2). The first is "any disruption in the continued success of established CF franchise." The second is a "clinical/regulatory setback on key pipeline programs namely povetacicept or inaxaplin."
- **Downside scenario.** The downside scenario lists "Failure to secure CF regulatory approvals and/or reimbursements in ex-US territories" (p.2). It also lists "Povetacicept/inaxapllin unable to generate meaningful revenues due to clinical/regulatory setbacks or competitive pressure" (p.2). The misspelling "inaxapllin" is in the original.
- **View on efficacy outside APOL1-FSGS.** The report gives no separate view on non-FSGS efficacy.
  - It titles its Phase 2a slide "Inaxaplin Demonstrated Clear Clinical PoC in AMKD" (p.11).
  - It writes that the Phase 2a "is small (N=13) with short treatment duration (13-week)" but "provided encouraging clinical evidence" (p.11).
  - The slide reports "~50% mean reduction in UPCR at 13-week with inaxaplin treatment" and cites "Egbuna et al., NEJM 2025" (p.11).
  - It describes inaxaplin as inhibiting "APOL1 protein-mediated induction of channel formation in podocytes" (p.11).
  - The report notes that AMPLITUDE enrolls a broader population than the FSGS-only Phase 2a (p.12). It does not comment on how efficacy may differ in that broader population.
